Understand the method

How support reactions are found

The solver is useful only if the free-body diagram and sign convention are correct.

1. Replace supports with reactions

A 2D pin support can supply horizontal and vertical reactions, Aₓ and Aᵧ. A roller on a horizontal surface supplies one vertical reaction, Bᵧ.

ΣFₓ = 0

2. Use moment equilibrium

Taking moments about A removes Aₓ and Aᵧ from the moment equation. Solve that equation first to obtain Bᵧ, then use vertical-force equilibrium to find Aᵧ.

ΣMₐ = 0 → Bᵧ   |   ΣFᵧ = 0 → Aᵧ
